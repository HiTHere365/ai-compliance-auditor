Meridian Support Assistant: System Capability Overview

This is a fictional sample document. Meridian Systems, its products, and the
figures below are invented for demonstration purposes only.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

The Meridian Support Assistant is a customer-service chatbot deployed on the
Meridian Systems help center and inside the Meridian mobile application. It
answers billing questions, walks customers through device troubleshooting,
and opens or updates support tickets on the customer's behalf. It handles
first-line support for consumer and small-business accounts in English and
Spanish.

## 2. Model Access

The assistant is built on a hosted general-purpose large language model
accessed through a vendor API over TLS. Meridian does not fine-tune the base
model. Behavior is controlled through a versioned system prompt and a
retrieval layer that supplies approved help-center articles as context.

Access to the vendor API is restricted to the assistant's backend service
account. API credentials are stored in the company secrets manager and
rotated every 90 days. Product staff cannot call the model directly; all
traffic passes through the assistant backend, which enforces a per-customer
rate limit of 60 messages per hour.

## 3. Tool Use

The assistant can invoke the following internal tools during a conversation:

- lookup_account: reads account status, plan, and last three invoices for the
  authenticated customer only.
- create_ticket: opens a support ticket in the Meridian ticketing system with
  the conversation summary attached.
- update_ticket: appends notes to an existing ticket owned by the customer.
- schedule_callback: books a phone callback slot with a human agent.
- knowledge_search: retrieves approved help-center articles.

The assistant cannot issue refunds, change billing details, cancel service,
or access accounts other than the one authenticated in the current session.
Tool calls that would modify account data beyond ticket notes are rejected by
the backend policy layer regardless of model output.

## 4. Data Handling and Retention

Conversation transcripts are retained for 30 days to support ticket follow-up
and are then deleted. Transcripts are stored encrypted at rest in the
Meridian customer-data region and are not shared with the model vendor beyond
the API request itself. The vendor contract specifies that prompts and
completions are not retained by the vendor or used for model training.

Customers can request deletion of their transcripts earlier than 30 days
through the privacy request form. Account numbers and payment card fragments
are masked before any text is sent to the model.

## 5. Human Escalation

The assistant hands the conversation to a human agent when any of the
following occur:

- The customer asks for a person, uses words such as "agent", "human", or
  "representative", or expresses frustration in two consecutive messages.
- The conversation exceeds eight turns without resolving the issue.
- The customer mentions a safety issue with a device, a legal claim, or a
  request involving a deceased account holder.
- The assistant's confidence in the retrieved articles falls below the
  configured threshold.

During business hours the handoff is a live chat transfer; outside business
hours the assistant books a callback and creates a ticket. The full
conversation is visible to the receiving agent.

## 6. Access Control and Change Management

Changes to the system prompt, tool definitions, or retrieval corpus require a
pull request approved by one member of the Support Engineering team and one
member of the Trust and Safety team. Releases follow the standard monthly
application release train, with an emergency path for content corrections.

## 7. Known Limitations

The assistant may misunderstand mixed-language messages and does not support
voice input. It relies on the freshness of the help-center corpus, which is
reviewed quarterly. Answers about pricing are drawn only from published plan
pages and may lag promotional offers by up to one week.
